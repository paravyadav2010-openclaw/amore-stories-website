# Focus on systems over goals
Source: [[Grocery Run|Atomic Habits]]

## Explanation
Goals are about the results you want to achieve, while systems are about the processes that lead to those results. Winners and losers often have the same goals, so goals alone don't lead to success. Relying on goals restricts happiness until the outcome is achieved and can lead to quitting after the goal is met. Lasting change comes from building a system of continuous refinement.
