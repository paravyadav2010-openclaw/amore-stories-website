# Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement
Source: [[Hi how are you|Atomic Habits]]

## Explanation
Small, consistent improvements—just 1% better each day—can lead to remarkable transformation over time. Habits compound like financial interest: good habits multiply success, while bad habits multiply problems. A 1% daily improvement leads to being 37 times better after one year. This happens because small changes seem insignificant until you cross a critical threshold where results become visible.
