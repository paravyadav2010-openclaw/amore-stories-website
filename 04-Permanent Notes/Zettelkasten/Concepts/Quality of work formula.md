# Quality of work formula

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]

## Explanation

High-quality work = (Time Spent) × (Intensity of Focus). This means working intensely on one task for 2 hours beats working scattered across 8 hours. For my photography, deep editing for 90 minutes produces better results than checking email while casually editing all day. Intensity matters more than raw hours.
