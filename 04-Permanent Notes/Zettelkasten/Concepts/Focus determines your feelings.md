# Focus determines your feelings

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]

## Explanation

My circumstances don't control how I feel. My brain creates my experience based on what I choose to focus on. A day of deep photo editing feels rewarding even if challenging. A day of shallow admin work feels frustrating even if easy. What I pay attention to shapes my entire experience of work and life.
