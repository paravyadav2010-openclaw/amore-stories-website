# Undistracted focus is rare competitive advantage

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]

## Explanation

The ability to focus completely without distraction is becoming extremely rare but increasingly valuable. As digital distractions multiply, fewer people can sustain deep concentration for long periods. This makes deep focus my biggest competitive advantage in photography and business. If I can edit photos or plan sessions with deep focus while others scroll social media, I'll produce better work faster and stand out to clients.
