# Deep work creates real value

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]

## Explanation

Deep work produces real, lasting value that moves my work and business forward. Shallow work feels productive but creates little lasting value and is easy to replace. For my photography business, editing photos deeply or planning client sessions creates real value, while answering emails all day is shallow work that doesn't build my skill or business. Most of my time goes to shallow work. I need to shift more time to deep work that builds my photography skills and business.
