# Three levels of behaviour change

Source: [[Hi how are you|Atomic Habits]]
## Explanation

There are three levels of behaviour change: outcomes (what you achieve), processes (what you do), and identity (who you become). Most people work inside-out (start with outcomes) which is why they fail. The lasting approach is outside-in: first decide who you want to become (identity), then design processes that reflect that identity, then achieve outcomes naturally. For my photography business, I should start with "I am a professional who does deep work" not "I want to earn $X".
