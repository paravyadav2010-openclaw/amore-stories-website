# Behaviour change is identity change

Source: [[Atomic Habits – James Clear]]

## Explanation

Real behaviour change is identity change. I can't just add a habit; I must become a new person through that habit. To build a deep work habit, I don't just schedule focus time. I become someone who does deep work. To build a photography business, I don't just take photos. I become a professional photographer. This shift from "I want to do this" to "I am this person" is what makes habits stick for life.
