# Environment design is more powerful than motivation
Source: [[Hi how are you|Atomic Habits]]

## Explanation
Motivation is overrated; environment often matters more. Small, strategic changes in your physical and social surroundings can lead to massive shifts in behaviour over time. By making cues for good habits visible and cues for bad habits invisible, you make good habits automatic and bad habits harder to trigger, without relying on willpower.
