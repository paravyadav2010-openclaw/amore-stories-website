# Habits Are Proof of Identity

Source: [[Hi how are you|Atomic Habits]]

## Explanation

Habits don't just change what I do; they change who I am. Each time I do deep editing work, I reinforce my identity as a serious photographer. Each time I skip editing to scroll social media, I reinforce my identity as someone distracted. Habits are how I prove to myself (and the world) who I actually am. Every small action is a vote for the person I want to become.

**Core Insight:** Habits aren't just actions—they're votes for your identity.

**The Feedback Loop:**
Identity → Behavior → Stronger Identity

**Key Principle:** You don't need perfection. You need more votes FOR than AGAINST.
