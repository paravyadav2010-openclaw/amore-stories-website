# Philosophical argument for deep work

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]

## Explanation

Deep work helps me produce excellent work and find meaning through mastery. The craftsman approach means meaning comes from building skill and excellence, not from external validation. As I master photography through deep practice, I naturally find purpose in my craft without needing to search for it.
