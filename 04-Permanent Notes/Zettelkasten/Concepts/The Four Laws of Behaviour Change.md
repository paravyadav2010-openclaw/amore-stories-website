# The Four Laws of Behaviour Change
Source: [[Hi how are you|Atomic Habits]]
## Explanation
The process of building a habit can be divided into four simple steps: cue, craving, response, and reward. These steps are the backbone of every habit, and they give us a framework for change. The Four Laws of Behaviour Change are a set of rules we can use to build better habits. They are: 1. Make it obvious, 2. Make it attractive, 3. Make it easy, and 4. Make it satisfying.
