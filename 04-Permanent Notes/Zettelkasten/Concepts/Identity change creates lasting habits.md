# Identity change creates lasting habits

Source: [[Atomic Habits – James Clear]]

## Explanation

Lasting behaviour change comes from changing your identity, not just setting goals. If I want to run marathons, I shouldn't focus on the goal "run a marathon". Instead, I should become a runner first. The same applies to photography: instead of "take 10 client sessions this month", I should become "a professional photographer who does excellent work". When identity shifts, habits follow naturally and last.
