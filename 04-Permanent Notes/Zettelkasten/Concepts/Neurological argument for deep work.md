# Neurological argument for deep work

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]
## Explanation

What I pay attention to determines how I feel, not my external circumstances. Focusing on meaningful challenges creates satisfaction and engagement. Focusing on shallow distractions creates dissatisfaction. Deep work trains my brain to pay attention to valuable things, improving both my work and mood.
