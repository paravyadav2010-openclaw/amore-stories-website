# Three groups thrive in modern economy

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]

## Explanation

The current economy strongly advantages three groups: high-skilled workers who master complex technology, superstars who are the best in their field, and owners with capital to invest in technology. Most people don't fit any of these categories. To join any group, I need rare skills or excellence, both of which require deep work to develop. As a photographer, I need to become either a high-skilled specialist in editing/lighting or a superstar in my niche to thrive long-term.
