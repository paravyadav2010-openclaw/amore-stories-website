# Attention control creates good life

Source: [[Deep Work – Cal Newport]]

## Explanation

Controlling what I pay attention to is the most important skill for living a good life. Winifred Gallagher found that attention management improves every part of experience. For me, the more time I spend on social media, the more tired, guilt, and like a waste of time I feel.
