# Act like the person you want to become

Source: [[Hi how are you|Atomic Habits]]

## Explanation

Decide who you want to become, then think about what that person would do. If I want to become a fit, healthy person, I ask: what would a fit person do? Go to the gym. What would an unhealthy person do? Watch TV. For my photography, I ask: what would an excellent photographer do? Deep editing work. Schedule client time. Study lighting. When I act like that person, I become that person.
