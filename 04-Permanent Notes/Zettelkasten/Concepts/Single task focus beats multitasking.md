# Single task focus beats multitasking

Source: [[Deep Work - Cal Newport|Deep Work]]

## Explanation

Working intensively on one task produces better results than spreading attention across multiple tasks. When I focus completely on editing one client's photos, I do higher quality work faster. If I edit while answering emails or checking social media, everything suffers. Single-task focus is how I build my photography skills and reputation.
