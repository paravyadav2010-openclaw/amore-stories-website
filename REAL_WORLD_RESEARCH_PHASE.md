## Decision Framework

I'll score each opportunity on 8 criteria:

1. **Time to First Money** (0-1 months, 1-2 months, 2+ months)
2. **Startup Cost** ($0, $5k, $10k, $20k)
3. **Monthly Income Potential** (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive)
4. **Profit Margin** (0-20%, 20-50%, 50-80%)
5. **Competition Level** (Low, Moderate, High, Extreme)
6. **Scalability** (Limited, Moderate, High, Unlimited)
7. **Risk** (Low, Medium, High, Extreme)
8. **My Execution Capability** (High, Medium, Low)

**Score Calculation:**
```
Opportunity Score = (Time × 2) + (Income × 3) + (Margin × 2) - (Cost × 1) - (Competition × 1) - (Risk × 1)
```

Higher score = better opportunity.
