# 💰 Business Model & Monetization

## The Core Idea

**What:** Automated rental property discovery service
**Who:** Renters in Melbourne (expanding to other cities)
**How:** Daily/weekly newsletter with curated property picks
**Why:** Finding rentals is painful; time is money

---

## Revenue Streams

### Tier 1: Free (Entry)

**What they get:**
- Top 5 picks per week
- One city (Melbourne)
- Basic filters (price, location)

**What you get:**
- Build email list
- Build Telegram channel
- Establish brand
- Get feedback

**Cost:** Free
**Revenue:** $0
**Purpose:** Audience building

---

### Tier 2: Premium - Basic ($5/month)

**What they get:**
- All property picks (not just top 5)
- Daily updates (vs weekly)
- Custom filters (max price, suburbs)
- Email delivery (not just Telegram)

**What you get:**
- Recurring revenue
- Loyal customer base
- Data on preferences

**Revenue:** $5 × subscribers/month

---

### Tier 3: Premium - Pro ($10/month)

**What they get:**
- Everything in Basic
- Multiple suburbs (anywhere in Melbourne)
- Early access (1 hour before free tier)
- Instant alerts (new listings matching criteria)
- Detailed analytics (price trends, availability)

**What you get:**
- Higher revenue per customer
- Most engaged users
- Product validation

**Revenue:** $10 × subscribers/month

---

### Tier 4: Enterprise ($25/month)

**Target:** Real estate agents, property managers

**What they get:**
- API access
- Bulk property alerts
- Market insights
- Competitor tracking
- Custom reports

**What you get:**
- High-value B2B customers
- Scalable revenue
- Partnership opportunities

**Revenue:** $25 × business subscribers/month

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Launch (Weeks 1-4)

**Goal:** 100 free subscribers

**Tactics:**
- Post in Melbourne Facebook groups ("Renters in Melbourne")
- Reddit: r/melbourne, r/australia
- Twitter/X: Share picks with hashtags
- WhatsApp groups: Local communities

**Cost:** $0
**Time:** 5-10 hours/week

---

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 2-6)

**Goal:** 500 free, 25 premium subscribers

**Tactics:**
- Launch website with subscription form
- Add referral program (invite 3 friends = 1 month free)
- Partner with relocation services
- Content marketing: "Melbourne Renting Guide" blog posts
- SEO: "rental properties [suburb]" keywords

**Cost:** $50-100/month (hosting, domain)
**Revenue:** $125/month

---

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 7-12)

**Goal:** 1,000 free, 50 premium subscribers

**Tactics:**
- Paid ads (Google, Meta): Target by location + interests
- Influencer partnerships (Melbourne real estate accounts)
- Press coverage: "AI-powered rental finder"
- Expand to Sydney, Brisbane

**Cost:** $200-500/month (ads + tools)
**Revenue:** $250-500/month

## Competitive Advantage

### What Makes This Different?

1. **Automation:** 100% automated scanning (no manual work)
2. **Speed:** Alerts within minutes of new listings
3. **Quality:** AI-scoring for value (price/location ratio)
4. **Focus:** Melbourne-specific expertise
5. **Simplicity:** Just the best picks, no overwhelm

### Competitors

| Competitor | Pros | Cons |
|------------|------|------|
| Domain.com.au | All listings | No filtering, overwhelming |
| Flatmates.com.au | Good for shares | Not for full properties |
| Facebook Groups | Real-time | Manual, no scoring |
| Real Estate Agents | Full service | Expensive, limited inventory |

## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Conversion

**Mitigation:**
- Offer 7-day free trial for premium
- Show value before asking for payment
- Constantly improve property quality

### Risk 2: Competition

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on Melbourne (local expertise)
- Build brand loyalty (reliability + quality)
- Move fast (first-mover advantage)

### Risk 3: Platform Changes (Domain/Trade Me)

**Mitigation:**
- Multiple sources (Domain + Trade Me + others)
- Agent Browser CLI (harder to block than APIs)
- Constant monitoring + quick fixes

### Risk 4: Churn (premium cancel)

**Mitigation:**
- Annual billing (cheaper, stickier)
- Ongoing value (daily updates)
- Community (Telegram channel, engagement)
