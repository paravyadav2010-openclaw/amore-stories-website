# Posing Guide – Family Sessions

## General Principles

- Start with simple standing poses to warm up
- Use movement to keep kids engaged (walking, running, jumping)
- Always check hands, chins, and gaps between bodies
- Natural light + positioning towards light source = better skin tones
- Take multiple shots of the same pose; movement creates better images

## Go-To Poses

### Pose 1: Walking Towards Camera

Family holds hands and walks slowly towards camera. Works for all ages. Creates movement and natural smiles.

**Setup:**
- Stagger heights (parents in middle or back)
- Have them walk 2–3 times, capture the best moment
- Works best on paths or open spaces

### Pose 2: Seated Parents, Children Leaning

Parents sit on grass or bench. Kids lean on shoulders or stand beside them.

**Setup:**
- Ensure parents are comfortable
- Kids can be playful (hugging, tickling)
- Creates intimacy and laughter

### Pose 3: Kids Running, Parents in Background

Kids run towards camera, parents follow behind.

**Setup:**
- Safety first – clear path
- Get low for kids' height
- Capture genuine joy and movement

### Pose 4: Close-Up Cuddle Huddle

Everyone close together, foreheads almost touching, looking down or at camera.

**Setup:**
- Creates intimacy
- Works for final shot of session
- Capture joy and connection

## Lighting & Timing Tips

- Golden hour (1 hour before sunset) = best light for portraits
- Back-lit poses create beautiful rim lighting
- Avoid harsh midday sun (move to shade)
- Overcast days = soft, even light (great for skin)
