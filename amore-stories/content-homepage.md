## Value Propositions

### Core Value #1: Natural & Authentic
**"Real Moments, No Posing"**

We don't stage. We don't direct. We document. Our photography style captures your wedding day exactly as it unfolds — the spontaneous laughter, the emotional embraces, the quiet glances that say everything. Your photos will look like *you*, because they *are* you.

### Core Value #2: Storytelling First
**"Every Photo Tells Your Story"**

We're not just taking pretty pictures — we're crafting a visual narrative of your love. From the nervous excitement of getting ready to the tearful first dance, every image contributes to the story of your day. Years from now, looking through your wedding gallery will feel like reliving your favorite memories.

**"Feeling is More Important than Flawless"**

A tearful embrace matters more than a perfectly symmetrical bouquet toss. A genuine laugh beats a forced smile every time. We prioritize authentic emotion over technical perfection, because photos that move you are the ones you'll treasure forever.

**"Be Yourself — That's What We Love"**

We create a comfortable, low-pressure environment where you can just be you. No awkward posing, no pressure to perform. Our couples often tell us they forgot we were there — until they saw the stunning photos we captured of them being themselves.

### Core Value #5: Timeless Quality
**"Investment in Forever"**

Your wedding photos will be treasured by generations. We deliver beautifully edited, high-quality images that stand the test of time. No trendy filters, no heavy editing — just natural, timeless photos that look as stunning 50 years from now as they do today.

## Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Core Brand Attributes
- **Warm:** Approachable, friendly, genuinely caring
- **Professional:** Reliable, organized, detail-oriented
- **Romantic:** Emotion-driven, love-focused, heartfelt
- **Authentic:** Real, honest, unpretentious

### Tone Rules
1. **Avoid corporate language** — no "synergize," "leverage," "optimize"
2. **Use contractions naturally** — "we're," "you'll," "can't"
3. **Write like you're talking to a friend** — warm, conversational
4. **Focus on emotion** — how photos make you *feel*, not just technical details
5. **Be humble** — celebrate couples, don't brag about yourself
6. **Use "we" and "you"** — create connection, not distance

### Words & Phrases to USE
- Love story
- Authentic moments
- Natural
- Unposed
- Genuine
- Emotion
- Connection
- Celebrate
- Privilege
- Capture
- Relive
- Treasured
- Memories
- You (couple-focused language)
- Your love
- Your story
- Your day

### Words & Phrases to AVOID
- "The best wedding photography"
- "Premium," "luxury," "exclusive"
- "Packages starting from" (use "investments starting from")
- "Professional photographer" (obvious, use "photographer")
- "High-quality" (obvious, show don't tell)
- "Perfect wedding" (real weddings are beautiful *because* they're not perfect)
- "Stunning" (overused, use specific, evocative language instead)

## Photography Style Guide

### Visual Style
- **Editing:** Natural, minimal, timeless
- **Color Palette:** Warm tones, true-to-life colors, no heavy filters
- **Composition:** Candid, dynamic, storytelling-focused
- **Light:** Natural light preferred, authentic atmosphere

### Content Priorities
1. **Emotional moments** (crying, laughing, embracing)
2. **Candid interactions** (between couple, family, friends)
3. **Details** (rings, bouquet, decor — but always with people context)
4. **Environmental shots** (venue, atmosphere, setting the scene)
5. **Portraits** (only when natural, relaxed, and brief)

### What We DON'T Do
- Forced family lineups (keep to minimum, make quick)
- Staged couple portraits with awkward posing
- Gimmicky shots (grooms jumping, bridesmaids synchronized)
- Over-edited, filtered images
- Trendy styles that date quickly

## Target Audience Profile

### Ideal Couples
- **Age:** 25-40
- **Style:** Relaxed, authentic, value genuine moments over Pinterest perfection
- **Personality:** Creative, thoughtful, prioritizes connection over appearances
- **Values:** Love, authenticity, storytelling, family
- **Budget:** Mid-to-high (value investment over lowest price)
- **Decision Factors:** Connection with photographer, portfolio style, personality match

### What Our Couples Are Looking For
1. Photos that feel like *them* — not a generic wedding template
2. A photographer they feel comfortable around (low-pressure experience)
3. Authentic moments captured, not staged poses
4. Images that tell their love story
5. Professionalism and reliability (trust is everything)

### Pain Points We Address
- *"We hate posing for photos"*
- *"We want photos that actually look like us"*
- *"We're nervous/awkward in front of cameras"*
