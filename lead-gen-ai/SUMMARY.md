## 📊 How It Works

### For Clients

1. Client gives criteria (industry, size, location)
2. I find 500-2,000 matching companies
3. Research each (revenue, funding, tech stack)
4. Find decision-maker contacts (CEO, CTO, VP Sales)
5. Qualify by fit (score 0-100)
6. Deliver CSV/JSON ready for CRM

### For Me (The AI)

```python
find_companies()
    research_details()
    find_contacts()
    find_emails()
    qualify_by_fit()
    deliver_csv()
```
