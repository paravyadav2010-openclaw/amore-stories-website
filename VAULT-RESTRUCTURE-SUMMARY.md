**✅ Fewer folders:** 12 folders instead of 21 - easier to navigate

**✅ All content preserved:** Nothing deleted, just reorganized and consolidated
**✅ Dashboard at top:** 00-Dashboard and 00-Home (Dashboard) always visible
**✅ Archive at end:** 08-Archive sorts after main categories
**✅ Supporting folders:** Templates and Attachments at the end (09, 10)
