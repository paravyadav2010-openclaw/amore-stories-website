## 🏆 Top 5 Quick Breakfasts (Under 15 Minutes)

### 1. 🥞 Besan Chilla (15 min total)
**Perfect for:** Busy mornings, healthy option
- **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min
- **Ingredients:** Besan, onion, tomato, green chilli
- **Toddler Tip:** No green chilli, make small chillas, serve with curd
- **Best served with:** Curd, green chutney, pickle

**Quick Steps:**
1. Mix besan with water to make batter
2. Add chopped onions, tomatoes, chilli
3. Heat pan, pour batter, spread thin
4. Cook both sides until golden
5. Garnish with coriander

---

### 2. 🍞 Bread Upma (15 min total)
**Perfect for:** Using leftover bread, very quick
- **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min
- **Ingredients:** Bread slices, onion, tomato, green chilli
- **Toddler Tip:** Mash bread pieces well, skip green chilli, add extra veggies
- **Best served with:** Curd, pickle

**Quick Steps:**
1. Cut bread into small pieces
2. Heat oil, add mustard seeds
3. Add chopped onions, sauté
4. Add tomatoes, turmeric
5. Add bread pieces, mix well
6. Cook 2-3 minutes, garnish with coriander

---

### 3. 🍳 Bread Omelette (13 min total)
**Perfect for:** Protein-rich breakfast, very fast
- **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 8 min
- **Ingredients:** Eggs, bread slices, onion, green chilli
- **Toddler Tip:** Scramble eggs instead of omelette, remove green chilli
- **Best served with:** Toast, chutney, ketchup

**Quick Steps:**
1. Beat eggs with salt and pepper
2. Add chopped onions, chilli
3. Heat butter, pour egg mixture
4. Cook until set
5. Toast bread slices
6. Serve omelette with toast

---

### 4. 🍯 Suji Halwa (15 min total)
**Perfect for:** Toddler favorite, sweet breakfast
- **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min
- **Ingredients:** Semolina/sooji, ghee, water, sugar, cardamom
- **Toddler Tip:** Perfect for toddler! Adjust sugar, add more ghee for energy
- **Best served with:** Warm, as is

**Quick Steps:**
1. Heat ghee, roast sooji until golden
2. Add water, mix well
3. Add sugar, stir
4. Add cardamom, cashews, raisins
5. Cook until thick, serve warm

---

### 5. 🥣 Masala Oats (15 min total)
**Perfect for:** Healthy breakfast, very nutritious
- **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 10 min
- **Ingredients:** Oats, onion, tomato, green chilli, peas, turmeric
- **Toddler Tip:** Mash oats well, reduce turmeric, skip green chilli
- **Best served with:** Curd, fruit on side

**Quick Steps:**
1. Heat oil, add cumin
2. Add chopped onions, sauté
3. Add tomatoes, chilli, turmeric
4. Add peas
5. Add oats, mix
6. Add water, cook until done
7. Garnish with coriander, serve hot

## 🥫 Make Ahead Options

### Night Before Prep

**Besan Chilla:**
- Mix batter, keep in fridge
- Add onions in morning

**Bread Upma:**
- Cut bread pieces
- Chop vegetables

**Rava Dosa:**
- Mix sooji with curd, keep overnight
- Ready to cook in morning!

**Suji Halwa:**
- Roast sooji in advance
- Just add water and sugar in morning
