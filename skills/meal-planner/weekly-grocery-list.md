## ⏱️ MEAL PREP TIPS

### Sunday Prep (1-2 hours)
1. **Soak chickpeas** for Chole Masala (Tuesday lunch)
2. **Prepare ginger-garlic paste** (or buy ready-made)
3. **Wash and chop vegetables**:
   - Onions (store in airtight container)
   - Tomatoes (chop or puree)
   - Mixed vegetables for pulao

### Daily Prep
- **Breakfast**: 10-15 minutes prep time
- **Lunch**: Can be prepped the night before
- **Dinner**: 30-45 minutes (some dishes freeze well)

### Freezer-Friendly
- **Chicken Curry** — makes 2-3 servings, freeze extra
- **Paneer Butter Masala** — freezes well
- **Chole Masala** — freezes well
- **Rotis/Parathas** — freeze cooked or dough

## 👶 TODDLER-FRIENDLY TIPS

For your 2-year-old:
- **Reduce spices** in a portion before adding
- **Mash vegetables** (dal, curries) for easy eating
- **Cut food into small pieces**
- **Avoid green chillies** in toddler's portion
- **Serve with extra yogurt** for digestion
