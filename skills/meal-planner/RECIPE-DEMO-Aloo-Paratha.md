🍽️ **Aloo Paratha**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📋 **Ingredients:**
• Whole wheat flour
• Potato
• Cumin
• Red chilli powder
• Coriander
• Ghee

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

👨‍🍳 **Steps:**
  1. Boil and mash potatoes
  2. Add cumin, chilli powder, coriander
  3. Make small dough balls
  4. Roll out with potato stuffing
  5. Cook on tawa with ghee
  6. Flip and cook both sides

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⏱️  Prep: 20 min | Cook: 15 min
👥 Serves: 3
🏷️  Tags: breakfast, north-indian, vegetarian, toddler-friendly

👶 **Toddler Tips:**
Serve with curd, remove chilli, cut into small pieces
