🥑 **Avocado Toast with Poached Egg**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📋 **Ingredients:**
• Ripe avocado (1)
• Bread slices (2)
• Eggs (2)
• Salt (to taste)
• Black pepper (to taste)
• Lemon juice (1 tsp)
• Butter or oil (for poaching)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

👨‍🍳 **Steps:**
  1. Toast bread slices until golden brown
  2. Cut avocado in half, remove pit
  3. Scoop avocado flesh, mash with salt, pepper, lemon juice
  4. Heat water in shallow pan (about 2-3 inches deep)
  5. Create gentle swirl in water with spoon
  6. Crack egg into the swirl (keeps egg together)
  7. Poach for 3-4 minutes until white is set
  8. **Cook well-done** (yolk fully cooked, no runny) for safety
  9. Remove with slotted spoon, drain excess water
  10. Spread mashed avocado on toast
  11. Top with poached egg
  12. Season with extra salt and pepper
  13. Serve warm

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⏱️  **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** 8 min | **Total:** 13 min
👥 **Serves:** 2
🏷️  **Tags:** breakfast, quick, non-vegetarian, healthy, toddler-friendly
📚 **Source:** Family favorite

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

👶 **Toddler-Friendly Version:**
✅ **Perfect for toddler!**
• Egg is well-cooked (no runny yolk) — safer for kids
• Mash avocado extra well — easier to eat
• Cut toast into small pieces — bite-sized
• Serve warm but not hot
• Remove any excess pepper for mild taste
• Skip salt for under-2 toddler if needed

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💡 **Pro Tips:**
• Use **ripe avocado** — easy to mash, creamy texture
• **Well-cooked egg** — important for toddler safety
• **Lemon juice** — prevents avocado from browning
• **Gentle swirl** — keeps egg shape while poaching
• **Warm toast** — holds avocado better than cold toast

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🥗 **Variations:**
• Add **crumbled feta** — extra protein
• Top with **microgreens** — fresh crunch
• Add **red pepper flakes** — spicy kick (skip for toddler!)
• Use **sourdough bread** — more nutrients

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📊 **Why It's Perfect:**
• **Quick:** 13 minutes total
• **Healthy:** Avocado (healthy fats) + egg (protein)
• **Toddler-safe:** Well-cooked egg, mashed avocado
• **Nutritious:** Balanced breakfast with protein + healthy fats
• **Easy:** Simple steps, minimal ingredients
